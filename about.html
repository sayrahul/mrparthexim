Quality Philosophy

Confidence should be built with evidence.

We do not treat quality as a slogan. The applicable controls depend on the product, source, destination, buyer specification and agreed order scope.

Before commercial confirmation

  • Clarify grade, cut size or mesh and end application
  • Align physical, chemical and microbiological requirements
  • Review source-specific certificates and their validity where applicable
  • Agree sampling, laboratory testing and acceptance criteria
  • Confirm packing, marks, shelf life and storage conditions

Before shipment

  • Confirm approved specification and applicable batch documents
  • Coordinate agreed packing, labelling and quantity checks
  • Review required commercial and shipping documentation
  • Arrange third-party inspection where agreed and feasible
  • Record written approvals, deviations and release instructions
