SUPPLIER VERIFICATION CHECKLIST
Mr Parth Exim | Educational template. Adapt it to your own risk process.
Mark each item: Verified / Not verified / Not applicable.

LEGAL
[ ] Legal name matches on every document
[ ] Registered address confirmed
[ ] GST registration checked
[ ] IEC (Import Export Code) checked
[ ] FSSAI licence checked for name, address, status and validity
[ ] APEDA / RCMC registration checked, where applicable
[ ] Authorised contact person confirmed
[ ] Business role confirmed: manufacturer, processor, exporter or trader

FACTORY OR SOURCE
[ ] Processing facility name and address identified
[ ] Factory or remote video review completed, where feasible
[ ] Capacity suits the order size and timeline
[ ] Product range and form confirmed

QUALITY
[ ] Certificates checked for issuer, facility, scope and validity
[ ] Certificate scope covers the product being purchased
[ ] Sample received and evaluated
[ ] Batch COA reviewed for product, batch, date, method, limit and result
[ ] Laboratory and test methods agreed
[ ] Packing, shelf life and storage conditions agreed

BANKING AND COMMERCIAL
[ ] Bank beneficiary name matches the legal name
[ ] Bank details confirmed through a separate verified channel
[ ] Any change of bank details re-confirmed before payment
[ ] Price basis, Incoterm, payment terms and responsibilities agreed in writing

Educational information only. Not legal, customs, tax, regulatory or laboratory advice.
Confirm current requirements with relevant professionals and authorities.
